# XueLearn brand policy

This policy covers the **XueLearn** and **好会学** names when used as product
identifiers, and the XueLearn artwork in [BRAND_ASSETS.json](BRAND_ASSETS.json).
It does not claim that any mark is registered. Third-party marks belong to their
respective owners. The copyright license for the listed artwork is
[LICENSE-BRAND](LICENSE-BRAND); the source code remains [MIT](LICENSE).

## Permitted references and ordinary use

- Keep copyright notices and acknowledge the source. Truthful text such as
  "Based on XueLearn" or "Compatible with XueLearn" is allowed when accurate and
  when your own product identity is prominent.
- Discuss, review, compare or link to the official project. This policy does not
  restrict descriptive/nominative use or other rights provided by applicable law.
- Run the official app, including at work. Private learning, evaluation, testing
  and development are allowed under the brand asset license's private-use terms.
- Make noncommercial contributions, source mirrors and community demonstrations
  under the brand asset license. Clearly identify unofficial or modified copies.

## Third-party products and commercial use

Use your own product name and visual identity for a commercial fork or service.
Commercial use of the listed brand artwork requires prior written permission.
Do not use XueLearn or 好会学 as the name of your own product, app-store listing,
business, domain or account in a way that suggests it is official. Do not claim
endorsement, partnership or certification that has not been granted.

When rebranding a fork:

1. Replace the images listed in `BRAND_ASSETS.json`, including desktop icons,
   installer backgrounds, favicons and wordmarks.
2. Change visible app names, installer names, web-manifest names and localization
   strings to your own identity. Use identifiers and links appropriate to your
   product. These implementation files remain editable under MIT.
3. Retain the MIT copyright and permission notices and any third-party notices.
   Attribution in a notices/about page does not require calling your product
   XueLearn. Check the separate licenses of other media you choose to include.

Noncommercial modified distributions that retain permitted brand artwork must
prominently identify themselves as unofficial and modified. Noncommercial use
never permits impersonation of the maintainers.

## Permission

Send commercial brand requests to `contact@xue-learn.com` with the proposed name,
artwork, use and distribution method. Only express written permission expands
the brand license. No brand permission is needed to exercise rights in the MIT
code with your own branding; these terms do not add a noncommercial condition
to MIT.
